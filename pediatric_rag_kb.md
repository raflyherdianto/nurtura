## PART 1: DEVELOPMENTAL MILESTONES & HOME STIMULATION BY AGE (CDC + WHO/UNICEF CCD)

### [0–6 Months: Early Sensorimotor & Attachment]
* **CDC Core Milestones (2–4m):** Calms when spoken to; smiles socially; chuckles; makes cooing sounds ("oooo", "aahh"); holds head steady without support when held; pushes up onto elbows/forearms during tummy time; brings hands to mouth; swings arm at toys.
* **CDC Core Milestones (6m):** Knows familiar people; laughs; takes turns making sounds; blows "raspberries"; rolls from tummy to back; pushes up with straight arms on tummy; leans on hands to support sitting.
* **WHO/UNICEF CCD Home Stimulation:** 
  * Provide ways for the baby to see, hear, move arms/legs freely, and touch safe household objects.
  * Slowly move a colorful household item (e.g., clean plastic cup or bright cloth) from left to right for visual tracking.
  * Practice daily supervised tummy time with a rolled towel under the chest if needed, placing safe items just out of reach.

### [6–12 Months: Object Permanence, Babbling & Mobility]
* **CDC Core Milestones (9m):** Looks when name is called; reacts when caregiver leaves; smiles/laughs at peek-a-boo; makes "mamamama" and "babababa" sounds; lifts arms to be picked up; gets to a sitting position independently; moves objects from one hand to the other; uses fingers to "rake" food.
* **CDC Core Milestones (12m):** Plays pat-a-cake; waves "bye-bye"; calls a parent "mama" or "dada"; understands "no"; pulls up to stand; walks holding on to furniture (cruising); drinks from an open cup held by caregiver; picks up small items between thumb and pointer finger (pincer grasp).
* **WHO/UNICEF CCD Home Stimulation:**
  * Give safe household containers to bang, drop, and put small safe items into and take out of (e.g., plastic bowls and large wooden spoons).
  * Hide a favorite object under a cloth/towel for the baby to find (object permanence).
  * Respond to the baby's sounds and pointing by naming the object clearly.

### [12–24 Months: Toddlers, First Words & Fine Motor Exploration]
* **CDC Core Milestones (15–18m):** Copies other children while playing; claps when excited; points to ask for something or get help; tries to say 3 or more words besides "mama"/"dada"; follows 1-step directions without gestures (at 18m); takes a few steps to walking independently; stacks at least 2 small objects; tries to use a spoon; scribbles.
* **CDC Core Milestones (24m):** Notices when others are hurt or upset; points to things in a book; says at least 2 words together (e.g., "More milk"); points to at least 2 body parts; kicks a ball; runs; walks up a few stairs; eats with a spoon.
* **WHO/UNICEF CCD Home Stimulation:**
  * Give the child things to stack, put into containers, and take out (nested plastic cups, small cardboard boxes).
  * Ask simple questions and respond to the child's attempts to talk; play naming games with everyday household objects and body parts.

### [24–60 Months: Preschoolers, Complex Language & Coordination]
* **CDC Core Milestones (30m):** Plays next to other children; says about 50 words; says 2+ words with one action word ("Doggie run"); uses hands to twist things (doorknobs, lids); jumps off the ground with both feet; turns book pages one at a time.
* **CDC Core Milestones (36m–60m):** Calms down within 10 minutes after caregiver leaves (3y); talks in back-and-forth conversation; asks "who/what/where/why" questions; strings items together (large beads/macaroni); draws a circle; puts on some clothes independently; hops on one foot (4y); tells a story with at least 2 events (5y).
* **WHO/UNICEF CCD Home Stimulation:**
  * Help the child count, name, and compare things in the house (sorting socks by color, counting spoons by size).
  * Create simple puzzle/matching games using cut cardboard shapes or household lids and containers.

## PART 2: COMPLEMENTARY FEEDING (MPASI) & ANTI-STUNTING GUIDELINES (WHO 2023 & IDAI)

### 1. Core WHO 2023 Recommendations (6–23 Months)
* **Timely Introduction:** Introduce complementary foods at 6 months (180 days) while continuing breastfeeding up to 2 years or beyond. (0–6 months is strictly exclusive breastfeeding/formula; NO solid foods or water).
* **Daily Animal-Source Foods (Crucial for Stunting Prevention):** Infants and young children (6–23m) MUST consume animal-source foods (meat, poultry, fish, liver, or eggs) daily to provide high-density bioavailable iron, zinc, and essential amino acids for linear growth. Plant proteins alone (tofu/tempeh) contain anti-nutrients and should not completely replace animal protein.
* **Healthy Fats & Energy Density:** Starchy staples alone provide low nutrient density. Meals must include healthy fats (e.g., unsalted butter, coconut milk, olive/canola oil, chicken fat/broth, avocado) to boost caloric density and support myelination/brain growth.
* **Dietary Diversity & Choking Safety:** Consume fruits and vegetables daily. Pulses, nuts, and seeds must be prepared in age-appropriate forms (finely ground/paste) to eliminate choking hazards.
* **Strictly Avoid:** Sugar-sweetened beverages, non-sugar sweeteners, honey (under 12m due to botulism risk), and excessive added salt/sugar.
* **Responsive Feeding:** Encourage autonomous self-feeding in response to hunger cues (reaching for spoon, opening mouth) and satiety cues (turning head away, closing mouth).

### 2. Age-Specific Food Texture & Frequency Matrix
* **6–8 Months (Transition Phase):**
  * **Texture:** Smooth thick puree (bubur saring kental) that does not easily drip off a tilted spoon, progressing to mashed foods.
  * **Frequency:** 2–3 main meals per day + 1–2 snacks (as needed) + continued breastfeeding.
* **9–11 Months (Oral-Motor Chewing Phase):**
  * **Texture:** Finely minced or chopped foods (bubur kasar / cincang halus), soft Mushy rice (nasi tim), and safe finger foods the infant can pick up.
  * **Frequency:** 3–4 main meals per day + 1–2 nutritious snacks + continued breastfeeding.
* **12–23 Months & Beyond (Family Food Phase):**
  * **Texture:** Family foods (makanan keluarga) cut into bite-sized pieces; avoid hard, round choking hazards (whole grapes, whole nuts, hard raw carrots).
  * **Frequency:** 3–4 main meals per day + 1–2 protein-rich snacks.
